INTRODUCTION:

Welcome to The Ink Gallery: a new Athkatlan establishment, and a place of many wonderful books. Read them, and let the tomes present you with epic battles and deadly struggles. Become the main hero/heroine of these books, even if only for a short moment, and face powerful enemies. Test your mettle and learn how to become even stronger than you already are. Remember, however, that there is a fee to be paid.

You may find The Ink Gallery behind the Council of Six building -- it may be reached either through its halls, or from outside, through the covered pathway right below the newly opened section of the area.

The mod is one of the Athkatlan Grounds mods, which is a series of smaller fan expansions created to make the main city of Baldur's Gate II even more interesting.



INSTALLATION AND COMPATIBILITY: 

As with any other WeiDU mod: unzip the archive into your Baldur's Gate II: Enhanced Edition directory and run Setup-InkGallery.exe. Note that this mod requires an up-to-date EE version of the game. Unlike most other Athkatlan Grounds mods, this one works only with the EE version; oBG2 and its iterations (like BGT) are not supported.

You need to start a new game for the mod to work properly (or at least use a savegame from before you've ever reached the Government District - if you don't, the new content won't show properly and may cause multiple problems).
